# Frey Ranch on Shopify with the AccelPay app (sales demo)

**Private sales demo.** Uses Frey Ranch's names, marks, product imagery and copy from https://shop.freyranch.com to show its Shopify store with the
AccelPay Shopify app installed in place of Flaviar Checkout. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Frey Ranch. Not a store; no order is placed.
Keep the link unlisted and share it 1:1 only.

Live: https://noshiftsherlock.github.io/freyranch-accelpay-demo/

Real (2026-11-07): products, images, copy and prices from the live store; network retailers with the product in stock
(127 routed in the demo, 131 carry the brand in total); Gopuff delivery ZIPs (496) from the
gopuff_feed snapshot (56 locations carry the brand). Illustrative: shipping, Gopuff fee, tax, payment step, confirmation.
